My work sits across education, policy, business, and public institutions, shaped by both academic training and hands-on institution building.

I studied Government, Statistics, and Women’s and Gender Studies at Georgetown University before completing my M.Ed. in Education Policy and Analysis at the Harvard Graduate School of Education, with additional study at Harvard Kennedy School and MIT.

Across research, institution building, entrepreneurship, and public engagement, education has remained a consistent thread, from policy and organizational work to learning, technology, and opportunity.

I am drawn to work that connects research with practice and leads to something tangible, from a policy idea to a program, partnership, or new initiative.

Academic Foundations

My academic background brings together politics, quantitative analysis, and education, with an interest in the legal and institutional structures that shape public systems. This foundation has shaped the way I approach questions of institutions, evidence, learning, and technology.

My work connects these perspectives across education, opportunity, and mobility, with particular attention to policy, institutional decision-making, and the relationship between education and broader social and economic change.

Across Education

My work in education spans public policy and institutional design, learning and technology, higher education, and pathways into work.
